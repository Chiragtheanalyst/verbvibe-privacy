Privacy Policy for VerbVibe

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Package ID: com.chiragshinde.verbvibe

Chirag Shinde ("we," "our," or "us") built the VerbVibe mobile application as an educational action-verb learning app designed for learners aged 7–14. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use VerbVibe.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

VerbVibe does not require account registration and does not operate external user-account servers. Your learning progress—including XP points, daily streaks, quiz scores, and audio preferences—is stored locally on your device using standard local storage and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

VerbVibe uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements. To deliver, measure, and prevent fraud on ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain technical information, including:

  • Device and Other Identifiers: App set ID and, where permitted by platform and age settings, the Android Advertising ID.
  • Approximate Location: General region estimated from the device's IP address (VerbVibe does not request GPS or precise location permissions).
  • User Product Interactions & Diagnostics: Ad views, taps, app launch events, and crash or performance diagnostics.

Learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Children's Privacy & Google Play Families Policy

Because VerbVibe is designed for learners aged 7–14, we configure Google AdMob for child-directed treatment (tagForChildDirectedTreatment and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent) with a maximum ad content rating of G (General Audiences).

  • Personalized or interest-based behavioral advertising is disabled.
  • Only Google Play Families self-certified ad formats appropriate for general audiences are requested.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal contact details (such as name, email address, or phone number) from children.

4. Managing Ad Preferences

Users and parents can manage or reset device advertising identifiers at any time through Android system settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or VerbVibe's privacy practices, contact the developer via the support email listed on our Google Play Store listing or through our GitHub repository at github.com/Chiragtheanalyst/verbvibe-privacy.
