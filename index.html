Privacy Policy for VerbVibe

Effective Date: November 23, 2026
Developer: Chirag Shinde

Chirag Shinde built the VerbVibe mobile application as a free educational app. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for anyone choosing to use VerbVibe.

1. Information Collection and Use

VerbVibe does not require user registration or login, and we do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location).

2. Local App Data

Your learning progress—including earned XP, ranks, daily streaks, and quiz scores—is stored locally on your device so the app can track your progress across sessions. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes this local data.

3. Audio, Reels & Internet Permissions

VerbVibe uses standard device Text-to-Speech (TTS) capabilities for verb pronunciation and standard internet access solely to load educational verb content and short verb reels. No personal user profiles or tracking identifiers are created during playback.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The current version of VerbVibe contains no third-party advertisements and does not sell or rent user data to third parties. If future updates introduce third-party services or advertising SDKs, this Privacy Policy will be updated prior to release.

5. Children's Privacy

VerbVibe is safe for learners of all ages. Because we do not collect personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us
